Good to know

Questions

How does payment work?

Nothing is charged when you send a request. We reply with your itemized total (the tree, 6.25% Massachusetts sales tax where it applies, delivery, and removal if you added it) and a secure link to pay by card. Your delivery day is held once you pay.

What if nobody is home?

Use the notes box on the form to tell us where to leave the tree, such as the porch, garage or beside the door. We’ll message you when we arrive. If we can’t reach you and there’s no place to leave it, we’ll reschedule.

What if my tree isn’t available?

We confirm availability before anything is charged. If the size or type you picked is sold out, we’ll offer the closest match. If it doesn’t suit you, you can cancel at no cost.

How does removal work?

Add it when you order and choose a pickup window. Before we come, please take off all lights, ornaments and tinsel, and leave the tree outside by your door. We pick up from outside only and never come inside. We’ll message you the day before, then haul the tree away.

What is your delivery policy?

When you order, you choose the size and type, and we deliver what you ordered. Please check your tree when it arrives. If it isn’t what you ordered, such as the wrong size or type, contact us within 24 hours with a photo and we’ll review it.

Trees are cut and sold as is, and no two are exactly alike. We can’t replace or refund a tree because of a change of mind, or its shape or look, once it has been delivered as ordered. You can change or cancel your order before we buy your tree, and we’ll confirm that when we send your total.

Can I pick a delivery time?

Yes. The form lists the days and time windows we have open. Pick one, and we’ll confirm it in our reply.

How big a tree can you deliver?

From 5 to 8 feet. Pick a tree about a foot shorter than your ceiling to leave room for the stand and topper.

Not in one of your towns?

Send a request anyway and we’ll tell you if we can reach you. Choose “Other” for your town.
